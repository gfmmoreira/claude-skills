## Pre-meeting prep

**Scenario:** PM preparing for a monthly skip-level. Three things to cover: getting ahead of a checkout conversion drop before it becomes a bigger stakeholder issue, escalating a pipeline blocker stuck for two weeks, and checking whether the referral program ownership is actually settled.

---

## 1-on-1 Prep, Rafael, 2026-04-14

**Relationship context:** Skip-level, monthly cadence, four sessions in. Rafael tends to acknowledge rather than react, hard to read where you actually stand. Conversations go better when you bring data and a specific ask, not just status updates.

**Your goal for this meeting:** Get Rafael to take action on the pipeline blocker, and get a read on whether the referral program is still yours before a decision is made without you.

---

### Agenda

**1. Checkout conversion**
Talking point: "The drop over the last three weeks is likely seasonal based on last year's pattern. Flagging it proactively in case it comes up from [stakeholder], so you have context if it does."
Your ask: Alignment, not action. You want him to know you're on top of it and have a position.
Watch for: If he pushes back on the seasonal explanation, he may have already heard something from the stakeholder. Don't defend, ask what he's heard.

**2. Pipeline blocker**
Talking point: "We've had a blocker sitting with the infra team for two weeks. I've followed up twice and it hasn't moved. I need help getting this escalated because it's starting to affect the delivery timeline."
Your ask: A name or a specific action by end of week, not "I'll look into it."
Watch for: A vague commitment. "I'll talk to them" without a timeline is not a resolution. Push for when.

**3. Referral program**
Talking point: "I want to check in on where the referral program sits. I've been running it but I'm not getting a clear signal on whether that's still the right setup going forward."
Your ask: Clarity on ownership. Is this still your workstream or is something changing?
Watch for: A non-answer. If he says "nothing's decided yet," ask when you should expect a decision.

---

### Things to hold back (for now)
- Frustration about the lack of feedback across previous sessions. Not the right conversation for a skip-level, and framing it as a complaint won't get you what you want.

### Open questions going in
- Has the stakeholder already escalated the checkout concern to Rafael, or are you getting ahead of it?
- Does Rafael know the referral program situation feels ambiguous from your side, or does he assume it's settled?
